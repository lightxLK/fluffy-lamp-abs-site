Tab 1: Single Point Loading (SPL)
Eyebrow: ONE DOCK, FULL ORDER, NO DELAY Title: Single Point Loading (SPL) Body:
Single Point Loading is a centralised system where all materials are prepared and loaded from one fixed point within our facility. It cuts dispatch time, reduces handling errors, and keeps vehicle movement smooth for a faster, more reliable delivery process.
Process:
 01 Order Received, order specification logged against live stock and mill schedule
 02 Scheduling, production and dispatch slotted into the loading calendar
 03 Material Prep, coil, profile, or sheet staged and inspected ahead of loading 
04 Loading, single-point loading onto the outbound vehicle, sequenced by drop 
05 Dispatch, vehicle cleared with documentation and tracked to site
